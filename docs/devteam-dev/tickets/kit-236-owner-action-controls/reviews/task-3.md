# Task review: T3 (the named records and their controls)

- Reviewer: task-reviewer (fresh subagent, claude-opus-5-5)
- Reviewed: e5d916b7979b58f756c44090c76bb3e641ad4043 (docs/devteam/dashboard/index.html: OA_ID, oaRecords, OA_SET, OA_CONFIRM, OA_PEEKS, oaState, oaRows, oaPaint, oaSig, questionCard, fetchPeek, render, tlCardBody, the [data-oa-know] and [data-oa-goal] click handlers, .oa-recs CSS)

I checked T3 against spec §1 to §3 and Edge cases, plan T3 and the carried minors. oaRecords reads only q.context and dedupes, in order. Real ids carry the writer suffix (g-…-supervisor), which OA_ID requires and matches. oaState follows the §2 table: knowledge from OA_SET, then the data, then the peek. Retired is done. Accepted is "Done: kept" only when the owner set it (OA_SET, or by === 'owner' from updates or set_by, the exact value writerClass uses) after asked_at. Anything else draws Retire it, plus Keep it for an owner lesson. Goals: abandoned or met is done, anything else draws Let go, and a goal missing from the data reads "no such record". The no-record arm (404) draws no control and never holds Done, and "checking…" holds it. OA_SET entries drop when the data or peek agrees, or on undo. [data-oa-know] posts the Knowledge page's body under k:<id>. [data-oa-goal] keeps its confirm in OA_CONFIRM for 6 s, so it survives redraws, then posts the hold bar's body under g:<id> with holdOverlay and loadFleet. Neither attribute matches the old [data-know] or [data-hold-id] handlers. The refusal path is wired: undo repaints, and press writes "Not saved" into e-<pre>oa-<qid>-<rid>, the row's own span. oaPaint rewrites [data-oa-recs] and [data-oa-anyway] in every copy and keeps focus through focusMark and focusBack. focusKey is built from data-oa-goal and data-oa-q, not the class, and nth keeps the same copy. A landed peek calls oaPaint, not a forced redraw. The thread signature carries oaSig. XSS: every value from data goes through esc. The href is encodeURIComponent of an id OA_ID has matched. No new const is read before its declaration at load. What remains are small leaks and transient display glitches.
