1. **A reused pid (scripted snapshots and signal, pids 9,000,001 and up).** Pids whose parent the walk never stopped are never signalled (1c); a child of a pid whose SIGSTOP failed (ESRCH) is never walked (1d); a stop that never lands (`D`, or a traced `t`) is not killed and is resumed (1g, 1h). A pid that is still the process the walk stopped can't be confused, since a stopped process can't exit. What gets through is reuse in the window between a snapshot and its SIGSTOP: then the unrelated process is left stopped (1a), or killed when its recorded parent has exited or ps failed (1b, 1e). B1. Not plausible at pid_max 4194304; a Minor.
