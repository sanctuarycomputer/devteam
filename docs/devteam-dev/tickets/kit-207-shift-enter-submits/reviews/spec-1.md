I read the spec against `docs/devteam/dashboard/index.html` (`questionCard` and its secret/sensor branches, `reviewCard`'s decline form, `tlQuestion`, which keeps review and secret cards out of a thread, the click handler's `[data-decline]`, `[data-reply]` and `[data-answer]` branches with the which-choice step, `press`/`answerPress`/`markAnswered`, `tlCardFrame`, `tlSend` and its keydown listener at 4983, and the keydown listeners at 3751, 4494 and 5279), against `test/dashboard.test.mjs` (every `p.key('Enter')`), and against `test/lib/chrome.mjs` (`key`, `hold`, `send`). The design is sound. One `document` keydown listener that clicks the card's own submit button reuses the which-choice step and every refusal. The button lookup is right: a thread copy is only ever a plain card with `[data-answer]`, and the sensor card's fallback to a newline is right too. `.click()` reaches the handler, which has no `isTrusted` guard. The Enter reversal follows from the ticket's wording. But the plan to migrate the existing tests miscounts the call sites. Two acceptance checks for done-when 3 would also pass with the guard they test removed, so they prove nothing.
