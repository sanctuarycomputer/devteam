## devteam

This repository is built by a supervised fleet of agent sessions using devteam. Before acting, read `docs/devteam/README.md` and the one doc for your role:

- spawned for a ticket (`DEVTEAM_TICKET` is set, or your branch is `ticket/<id>`): `docs/devteam/process/worker.md`
- the supervisor: `docs/devteam/process/supervisor.md`
- asked to review: `docs/devteam/governance/review.md`
- anything else: `docs/devteam/README.md`

Project-specific answers live in `docs/devteam/project/binding.md`. Rules: stay inside your ticket's `touches`; stop and ask rather than invent; workers never merge (ask the supervisor for the merge word with your exact head, and the supervisor merges).
