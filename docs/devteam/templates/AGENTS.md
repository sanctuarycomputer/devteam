## devteam

This repository is built by a supervised fleet of agent sessions using devteam. Before acting, read `docs/devteam/README.md` and the one doc for your role:

- spawned for a node (`DEVTEAM_NODE` is set, or your branch is `node/<id>`): `docs/devteam/process/worker.md`
- the supervisor: `docs/devteam/process/supervisor.md`
- asked to review: `docs/devteam/governance/review.md`
- anything else: `docs/devteam/README.md`

Project-specific answers live in `docs/devteam/project/binding.md`. Rules: stay inside your node's `touches`; stop and ask rather than invent; never merge without the supervisor's merge word for your exact head.
